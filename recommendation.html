9. Integrated Recommendation

Overall judgement

The five-store pilot is complete and technically validated, and the group recommends proceeding to enterprise rollout, subject to the open items below being settled first.

The evidence for that judgement is consistent across the artefacts rather than resting on one of them. All 13 work packages and five milestones closed on their baseline dates, giving a schedule variance of zero. Every quality target was met and UAT was signed off in full at milestone M2. All six registered risks were closed with funded responses, and the one that materialised during execution was absorbed inside the contingency without moving the critical path. Spend stayed inside the approved $106,986 with contingency remaining. On the benefit side, checkout is 35% faster and inventory now synchronises in real time, which is what the project was authorised to achieve.

The pilot is not, however, a proof that the platform scales. Everything above describes five stores, one cutover and one specialist carrying a third of the work. That distinction is what the next steps are built around.

How the artefacts connect

The recommendation above depends on the artefacts agreeing with each other. They connect as follows:

  • WBS to schedule. Work packages 1.1 to 5.2 appear as the linked task bars in the ProjectLibre Gantt, so every deliverable carries a date, a duration and a dependency.
  • Schedule to Trello. Each scheduled activity exists as a card titled with its WBS identifier, so daily board movement reports against the baseline instead of against opinion.
  • Stakeholders to risk. Cashiers sit in the high-interest, low-power quadrant, which is the profile for adoption resistance. That is why R2 exists and why its response is rehearsal rather than notification.
  • Risk to cost. The response to R1 was funded in CAT-03 at planning time and topped up from the CAT-05 contingency through CR-02 during execution.
  • Risk to issue to action. R1 materialised as ISS-02, latency at two stores. Because the response was pre-funded, the correction was a purchase inside contingency and the critical path never moved.
  • Change to quality. CR-01's hardware substitution created a re-test obligation, which surfaced as ISS-01 during UAT and was closed before the M2 sign-off gate.
  • Status to closure. The Excel status register is the source of the zero schedule variance reported at closure and of the benefits claimed against the original business problem.
